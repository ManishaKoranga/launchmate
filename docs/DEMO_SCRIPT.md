# Demo script (about 5 minutes)

**Setup before the demo:** log in to https://sitelaunch.duckdns.org with a demo account; have the sample
website https://sitelaunch.duckdns.org/s/homebakers-nainital open in a second window (mobile view works well).

1. **Problem (30 s).** "A home baker in Deoband sells cakes on WhatsApp. She has no website, and orders get lost."
2. **Onboarding by voice (60 s).** Tap the microphone and say, in Hinglish: "Main Deoband mein home bakery chalati hoon.
   Customised cakes, cupcakes aur brownies banati hoon." Show the transcript appearing in the box, then send.
   Point out the "What we understood" panel filling in, and the follow-up question for the missing details.
3. **Finish the details (30 s).** Give a name, a WhatsApp number and a price. Show the review step, then tap **Build my website**.
4. **Real progress (30 s).** Show the generation steps. Say that these are real backend steps, not a fake timer.
5. **Editor (60 s).** Edit the headline, switch to mobile preview, and publish.
6. **Customer view (45 s).** Open the public site in the second window, then send an inquiry from the contact form.
7. **Owner inbox (45 s).** Open **Inquiries**, show the new lead, change its status to *Contacted*, and tap WhatsApp.
8. **Architecture and safety (30 s).** Mention the modular monolith, the Claude output validation, cross-tenant
   tests, and the local speech-to-text.
9. **Close (15 s).** "Anyone with a phone can get a website and an inbox for their business, in their own language."
